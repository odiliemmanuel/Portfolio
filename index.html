About Me

Building impactful software.

I'm a Software Engineer at Semicolon Africa based in Lagos, Nigeria, where I lead the LearnQuest AI capstone team. I work across the full stack — from backend services and frontend interfaces to deployment pipelines and the data layer that informs product decisions.

I've led a few of my team members building EstateLink, a real-estate microservices platform, and I'm currently building an AI-powered learning platform, a real-time fintech transaction monitor, and a voice-first banking app for Nigerian users.

I'm passionate about building scalable, production-ready systems that look simple on the outside but are powerful under the hood.
